The program is structured to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world environmental action. By leveraging AI models and clean datasets, we analyze energy efficiency, optimize water resources, and design sustainable campus interventions.

Policies & Guidelines

Commitment & Professional Conduct

Interns must commit to the internship with consistent participation throughout the program. You are expected to maintain professionalism, integrity, and respect for all individuals associated with the program.

Confidentiality & Ethics

Interns must not disclose any proprietary or sensitive data, reports, or tools received during the internship without prior written consent from the 1M1B Internships. Ethical compliance is key; failure to meet responsibilities may result in termination.

Certification Criteria

An Internship Completion Certificate will be awarded only upon successful fulfillment of all project requirements and submission of assignments/documentation. Completing fewer than 40 work hours will disqualify the intern.

Program FAQs

Have questions about rules, stipend, credentials, or schedules? Browse these responses provided by the 1M1B Support team.

If your query isn't listed, please reach out to ojaswi@onemoneb.org or ask in the official community group.

🔍
1. Is there any fee to join the internship?
No. The internship is completely free of cost. There are no registration or hidden charges.
2. Will I receive a stipend for this internship?
No, this is an unpaid internship. However, you will receive valuable learning, project experience, digital credentials, and a certificate upon successful completion.
3. How will communication happen during the internship?
All communication will happen through your registered email ID and community groups. Please check your inbox and spam folder regularly for updates.
4. What is the first assignment or task?
Your first assignment is to complete the Guided Learning Experience using the course link. It is shared in the onboarding email. This is mandatory and forms part of your internship requirements.
5. Will I get a certificate?
Yes. Students who complete all requirements including assignments, learning modules, and projects will receive a co-certified digital certificate from Microsoft and the 1M1B Foundation. You are eligible for two certificates: one for the bootcamp and one for the completion of the Internship.
6. Is attendance compulsory?
Participation is mandatory for all key sessions. Assignments and project files must be completed and submitted on time to receive the certificates.
7. What is the duration of the internship?
The internship duration is typically six weeks, encompassing interactive learning sessions, workshops, and applied project development.
8. Do I need prior knowledge of AI or coding?
No prior experience is required. The internship modules are designed to support beginners, starting from fundamentals and moving step-by-step towards advanced tools.
9. What tools or technologies will be covered?
You will explore beginner-friendly AI concepts, Python data libraries (Pandas, NumPy), data visualization charts, and PowerBI dashboards focused on solving sustainability challenges.
10. Can I mention this experience on my resume?
Yes! You can list this internship along with your sustainability project work, technical skills learned (Power BI, AI, climate analysis), and certifications earned.
